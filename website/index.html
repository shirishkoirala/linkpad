A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you connect.

Does it work over the internet?

Linkpad connects over your local network. Keep your phone and computer on the same network; guest Wi-Fi or network isolation can prevent discovery.

Do I need an account?

No. Pair directly with your computer using its QR code. Linkpad has no ads or analytics, and does not send your input through a cloud service.

Can I see my computer’s screen on my phone?

Linkpad sends input to your computer. It does not stream its screen. Use your computer’s monitor or TV to see what you’re controlling.

Why does the Mac app need Accessibility?

macOS requires Accessibility permission to move the pointer and send keyboard input. Enable Linkpad in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility, then follow the app’s restart prompt.

What if my computer’s address changes?

Local discovery helps find your paired computer again when its IP address changes. Keep Linkpad Server running and allow local network access for Linkpad Remote on your phone.
