# Linkpad store description

## Short description

Control your computer with your phone’s touchpad, keyboard and remote.

## Long description

Turn your phone into a touchpad, keyboard and remote for your computer. Whether you’re at your desk or relaxing on the sofa, Linkpad keeps everyday controls within reach.

### Move naturally

Move the cursor with one finger, tap to click, scroll with two fingers and use a two-finger tap for right-click. Hold and drag to move windows or select items.

### Type and use shortcuts

Send text to your computer and access keys such as Enter, Escape, Ctrl, Alt, Shift and Windows/Command.

### Sit back and control

Use the directional pad and Enter button to navigate. Adjust volume or mute from the Remote screen on supported computers.

### Pair once, reconnect easily

Find nearby Linkpad computers on your local network and pair by scanning the QR code displayed on your desktop. Save multiple computers and switch between them.

### Make it feel right

Adjust cursor sensitivity and choose whether to keep your phone’s screen awake while connected.

### Local and private

Linkpad connects directly over your local network using encrypted connections and verified device pairing. No account or cloud service is required.

### Getting started

Install the Linkpad desktop server on your Mac or Linux computer, connect both devices to the same local network, and scan the desktop’s pairing code.

macOS requires Accessibility permission for input control. Linux input control currently requires an X11 desktop session. Linkpad controls your computer; it does not stream its screen.
